# The roster

Sixty-plus agents across twelve departments, plus a chief of staff, an executive assistant, a strategic coach, and a standing QA team. Each agent has one job, its own system prompt, and a scoped tool set. They are named and themed on purpose: a characterful, legible agent is far easier to reason about, trust, and debug than `agent_07`.

Functions are listed first; the persona that voices each one is in parentheses.

## Leadership layer

| Function | Role |
|---|---|
| **Chief of Staff** | Cross-department aggregation, daily brief, approval queue, dispatch routing. The single point of accountability. |
| **Executive Assistant** | Hour-by-hour task list, day-of nudges, quick lookups. Peer to the CoS, not under it. |
| **Strategic Coach** | Sits outside every pipeline. Reviews all agents for drift and contradiction, watches sessions for repeated friction, scans for unused platform capability. Proposes edits, never applies them. |

## Careers: the job-search pipeline (14)

| Function | Role |
|---|---|
| **Department Head** | Plans and audits the pipeline; owns the prepped queue; never dispatches (planners are not orchestrators). |
| **Strategy Lead & Committee Chair** | Bookends the pipeline: sets the positioning brief first, makes the final go/no-go last. |
| **Résumé Tailor** | Mirrors the job description's language where truthful; preserves a locked canonical format. |
| **ATS Auditor** | Independently scores keyword coverage; kicks back to the writer below a threshold. The auditor is never the author. |
| **Cover-Letter & Outreach Writer** | Three voice registers across cover letters, cold outreach, and connection notes. Drafts only. |
| **People & Company Intel** | Finds named recruiters, hiring managers, and warm paths; reads company priorities. |
| **Achievement Verifier** | The truthfulness gate. Flags fabrication, inflated metrics, and borrowed pedigree. Nothing ships without sign-off. |
| **Recruiter Simulator** | Reads the package the way a skeptical recruiter does in fifteen seconds, hunting for reasons to reject. |
| **Red-Team Critic** | Argues the worst-case interpretation: logical gaps, contradictions, interview trap-doors. |
| **Humanization Editor** | Rewrites so the output reads like a person wrote it: varied rhythm, broken parallelism, no machine tells. |
| **QC Reviewer** | A multi-gate validation pass (structure, ATS, format adherence, voice) before the committee. |
| **Dashboard Keeper** | The single writer for status flips; records applied dates, files, and provenance with hard validation. |
| **Follow-up Tracker** | Scores aging applications and outreach, drafts personalized nudges on a 1- and 2-week cadence. |
| **Interview Prep** | Cross-references the résumé against likely questions, builds STAR stories, predicts skepticism. |

## Research & Intelligence (3)

| Function | Role |
|---|---|
| **Department Head** | Deep-dive analyst on companies, people, and decisions. Reads everything, cites sources. |
| **One-Pager Writer** | Turns dense analysis into a clear executive brief. Where the head is rigor, this is readability. |
| **Macro Context** | Adds the zoom-out frame (history, power, the long game) when "why now" is load-bearing. |

## Finance (10)

| Function | Role |
|---|---|
| **Department Head** | Capital allocation, investment philosophy, risk posture. |
| **Bookkeeping & Cash Flow** | Monthly P&L, category spend, and budget tracking. Cites numbers, never invents them. |
| **Tax Strategy** | Loss-harvesting, retirement-account architecture, capital-gains posture. Drafts; never files; recommends a licensed CPA at the line. |
| **Education Coach** | Investing from zero (accounts, funding mechanics, first trades) explained at exactly the right level. |
| **Portfolio Operations** | Allocation, rebalancing plans, trade tickets. Drafts the mechanics; never executes. |
| **Head of Research** + four analysts | Macro/rates, tech/growth, quality/defensives, and a contrarian red-team: a full investment desk that debates before it concludes. |

## Super-User QA: the standing critics

A dozen domain critics, each modeled on a real authority in their field, walk every dashboard from a user's perspective and file tickets against anything that doesn't hold up. A QA program manager triages and routes them. The people who build the surfaces are never the people who grade them: the same principle that governs the résumé pipeline, applied to the tools.

---

*Personas are drawn from film and television for memorability and are not real individuals. The design point stands: a named, single-purpose agent is a unit you can actually hold in your head.*
